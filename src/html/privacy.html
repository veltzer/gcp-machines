Machines

Privacy

Machines is run by Mark Veltzer for his students: each student gets a virtual machine in the cloud for class. This page says what the site keeps about you.

What is stored

  • With Google or GitHub sign-in: the identifier that provider gives your account, your name, your email address and the address of your profile picture. The site never sees your password there and asks for no other access to your account.
  • With email: your email address and a salted hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • When your account was made and when you last signed in, and which machine is assigned to you.
  • Your machine itself, with the files you keep on it, until it is deleted.

The administrator sees the list of accounts and which machine each one uses. A cookie keeps you signed in; it is used for nothing else.

What is not done

No advertisements, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used for anything but signing you in and running your machine. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Where it is kept

The site, its database and the machines run on Google Cloud, in the United States.

Removing your account

Write to mark.veltzer@gmail.com and your account and machine are deleted.
